# Lamine-Yamal
Poster Concept

I created a concept so unique that it feels like the era of Lamine Yamal himself becoming the new youngest GOAT, just as Lionel Messi retires from international football with Argentina.

## What I concept
1. Spain won the World Cup 2026.
2. His first World Cup debut and first World Cup win at 19 years old.
3. His spain jersey number is 19.
4. Titles: Spain now has 2 stars on the jersey.
5. The crown shows Lamine Yamal was born to be a legend. As a baby Lamine Yamal was held in Lionel Messi's arms, which made history.
